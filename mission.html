Exploration is not a destination. It is a decision to keep asking, keep learning and keep moving the boundaries of what is possible.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

A PRESENCE.
A PURPOSE.
A PERSPECTIVE.

SELENE imagines a future where the Moon becomes a platform for discovery — not a distant trophy, but a place where knowledge can take root.

Our concept mission is built around three ambitions: expand scientific understanding, develop technologies for living beyond Earth, and create a responsible framework for sustained exploration.

It is a speculative design experience, not an operational spaceflight programme. The future described here is an invitation to imagine, question and design.

02 / A LONG VIEW
PHASE 01

ARRIVE

Demonstrate precision landing, surface mobility and autonomous systems in a challenging polar environment.

PHASE 02

LEARN

Map local geology, study permanently shadowed regions and investigate the distribution of lunar volatiles.

PHASE 03

ESTABLISH

Explore modular shelter concepts, dependable power and resource-aware surface operations.

PHASE 04

LOOK OUTWARD

Use what we learn on the Moon to inform safer, smarter journeys deeper into the solar system.
