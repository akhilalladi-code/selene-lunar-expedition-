SELENE / SYSTEMS OVERVIEW

MADE FOR
THE MOON.

Every system is a response to an unforgiving environment. Explore the ideas that shape our imagined expedition architecture.

01 / TRANSPORT

Precision arrival

Autonomous navigation, hazard awareness and carefully planned descent profiles are central to reaching a safe landing zone.

02 / SURFACE

Mobility & access

Pressurised and unpressurised rover concepts extend the reach of crews and robotic science across the lunar terrain.

03 / HABITATION

A place to stay

Modular habitat thinking combines shielding, thermal management, life-support redundancy and efficient use of interior space.

04 / POWER

Energy resilience

Solar generation, energy storage and distributed power systems help support operations through demanding lighting conditions.

05 / SCIENCE

Read the record

Lunar samples and in-situ measurements can reveal clues about planetary formation and the history of the Earth–Moon system.

06 / RESOURCES

Use what is there

Research into lunar regolith and polar volatiles may one day support more sustainable exploration — with careful environmental stewardship.
